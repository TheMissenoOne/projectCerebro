## Design Context

### Users
TTRPG players (both at-table and solo) and game masters. Used during game sessions and between sessions for character/NPC management.

### Brand Personality
Bold, comic-book-inspired, cinematic, powerful. "X-Men internal system" aesthetic - sleek, modern, thematic.

### Aesthetic Direction
Tom Muller X-Men style: clean lines, bold typography, dark theme with vibrant accents, cinematic feel. Not generic "fantasy" - distinctive super-hero/comic aesthetic.

### Design Principles
- Bold, powerful visual hierarchy
- Dark background with high-contrast accents (red, yellow, blue)
- Cinematic, not clinical
- Navigation should feel like accessing a secret database
